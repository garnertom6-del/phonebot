## Purpose

Set the direction of Prayers of Care Inc for three years, and show that the direction came from
evidence rather than from opinion.

CARF does not ask whether an agency has ambitions. It asks to see the plan, the **inputs the plan
was built from**, the review that happened afterwards, and what changed as a result. A plan with
goals but no documented inputs is the single most common way 1.C is missed, and it is why
section 3 of this document exists.

---

## How this plan is meant to be used

1. **Fill in section 3 first, not section 5.** Goals written before the inputs are collected are
   guesses. Goals written after them are strategy.
2. **Nothing in section 5 is adopted until it has a number and a name.** "Improve quality" is not
   an objective. "80% of plans show a community activity goal, checked monthly by the Program
   Supervisor" is.
3. **Review it quarterly in the leadership meeting** (MTG-01, agenda item 7) and record the review
   in those minutes. Review the whole plan annually and record what changed in section 8.
4. **Adopting it is a Decision Log entry.** Prayers of Care Inc has no board, so adoption and every
   later change goes into the LEAD-01 section G Decision Log, not just into this file.
5. **Tell people about it.** Section 9. A plan nobody has seen is not a plan.

## 5. Strategic goals

Every goal carries a **measurable objective, a responsible position, a target, and the resources
it needs**. Owners are named by **position**; the person holding each position is in the ORG-01
function assignment table, which stays the single source of truth.

Years are counted from the effective date on the cover — Year 1 is the first twelve months —
so this plan does not go stale the moment a date slips.

|---|---|---|
