## 2. Purpose of the role

Lead Prayers of Care Inc. Ensure that Community Integration services delivered through peer
support are safe, ethical, person-centered, lawfully delivered and accurately documented — and
that the agency can show it.

These are the functions of the job for the purposes of the Americans with Disabilities Act. A

### Leadership and planning
1. Set, model and uphold the mission, vision and values.
2. Own the strategic plan and its documented annual review.
3. Hold leadership meetings at least monthly, with minutes recording decisions, owners and dates.
4. Maintain the Decision Log required in place of board minutes (LEAD-01, section G).
5. Sponsor accreditation preparation. CARF prep lead: `[FILL IN — self, or a named person]`

### Program and quality
6. Ensure the written Community Integration program description matches what the agency
   actually does, and correct whichever is wrong when they diverge.
7. Protect the rights, privacy, dignity and accessibility of every person served.
8. Ensure performance is measured, analysed at least annually, acted on, and **reported back to
   staff and to the people served**.
9. Ensure incidents are reported, reviewed within the required timeframe, analysed for trends,

11. Ensure every peer support role has written competencies, boundaries and a disclosure plan.
12. Ensure supervision, competency assessment and annual appraisal happen on schedule.
13. Ensure no staff member delivers service before credential verification, orientation and
    competency are complete and documented.

### Compliance and risk
14. Maintain licences, certifications, enrollments and insurance. Types held:

## 4. What this role does NOT do

Stating the limits protects the agency as much as stating the duties.

- **Does not deliver peer support as a credentialed peer specialist** unless the CEO personally
  holds the state peer credential and is assessed as competent like any other staff member. If
  she does deliver direct service, she is subject to the same credential verification,
  supervision, competency assessment and documentation review as everyone else — and someone
  other than her must review her documentation. Delivers direct service? `[FILL IN — yes / no]`
- **Does not provide clinical services**, assessment, diagnosis or therapy.
- **Does not prescribe, dispense, administer, store or transport medication.** Prayers of Care
  Inc does not offer medication management.
- **Does not review or approve her own service documentation** where she delivers service.
- **Does not act as the sole route for complaints about herself.** See ORG-01 section 5.

## 5. Accurate representation of accreditation status

The original draft listed "no false accreditation claims" as a success measure. It belongs in
the job description as a duty, because it is the CEO who signs the marketing.

1. Until CARF accreditation is awarded, the agency **does not** describe itself as accredited,
   CARF-accredited, CARF-approved, CARF-certified, or as meeting CARF standards — in any
   material, on the website, in social media, in grant applications, in referral packets or
   verbally.
2. "Preparing for CARF accreditation" or "pursuing CARF accreditation" is accurate and may be
   used. Nothing stronger.
3. If accreditation is awarded, the agency uses the accreditation seal and wording **only** as
   CARF permits, naming the specific program accredited and the term, and stops using them the
   day the term ends or the status changes.
4. The CEO reviews every public material against this rule before it is published, and records
   the check on the ORG-01 publication table.

**Why this matters more than it looks:** claiming an accreditation the agency does not hold is
a misrepresentation to payers and to the people it serves. It is one of the few things that can
end an accreditation application outright, and it is also exactly the sort of statement a
competitor or a disgruntled former employee reports.
