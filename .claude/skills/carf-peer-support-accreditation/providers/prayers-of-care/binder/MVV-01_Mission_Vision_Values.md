## Purpose

State who we are and how we treat people. Guide hiring, peer practice, and Community
Integration services. Give staff, persons served, referral sources and surveyors one
consistent answer to "what is this agency for?"

---

## Mission

Prayers of Care walks with people in recovery as they build meaningful lives in the community.

We use peer support to help people choose their own goals, grow natural supports, and take
part in everyday community life with dignity and hope.

---

## Vision

Every person we serve has a valued role in their community — connected, supported, and free
to define their own recovery.

---

## Values, and what each one looks like in practice

A value a surveyor cannot observe is a slogan. Each value below carries the behaviour that
evidences it, and where that evidence lives.

| # | Value | What it means | What it looks like in practice | Where the evidence is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Person-centered choice** | The person leads their plan. | Goals are recorded in the person's own words, in quotation marks. A person may decline any service without losing others. | Person-centered plans; declines documented without pejorative language |
| 2 | **Hope and recovery** | Recovery is possible and looks different for each person. | Staff speak about futures, not deficits. No pathway is promoted as superior. | Progress notes; supervision notes |
| 3 | **Peer partnership** | Lived experience is a strength we honour and use ethically. | Self-disclosure is purposeful, planned in writing, and reviewed at every supervision. | Personal disclosure plans; supervision notes |
| 4 | **Belonging** | Community integration means real participation, not isolation. | Service happens in real community settings. Unpaid supports are counted and grown. | Progress notes showing location; Natural Supports Map |
| 5 | **Respect and inclusion** | We welcome people of all backgrounds, beliefs and identities. | See the Statement on Faith below. | Rights acknowledgments; grievance log |
| 6 | **Safety and integrity** | We protect rights, privacy and honest practice. | We document what happened, never what should have happened. | Record reviews; incident log |
| 7 | **Accountability** | We measure what matters and improve when we fall short. | Results are published to staff and to the people we serve, including the bad ones. | Annual performance analysis; distribution log |

## Statement on Faith

**Read this section carefully. Because of our name, a surveyor will ask about it, and so will
payers and referral sources.**

Prayers of Care is named for the care we give, not for a belief anyone is required to hold.

**Prayers of Care does not offer, provide, lead or host prayer, worship, devotional or
religious activity as any part of its services.** The name reflects the Owner's own values
about how people should be cared for. It does not describe a service the agency delivers.
That is the clearest and most defensible answer, and it is the true one.

The rest of this statement holds regardless:

1. **No person is ever required to pray, hold any belief, attend any religious activity, or
   participate in any spiritual practice** to receive services from Prayers of Care, to
   continue receiving them, or to receive any particular level of service.
2. **No service, referral, support or benefit is conditioned on a person's faith**, on their
   lack of faith, or on any change in either.
3. Spirituality is one of the life domains a person may choose to work on, because it matters
   to some people in recovery. **Staff follow the person's lead.** Staff do not introduce
   religious content, offer prayer, or share their own beliefs unless the person has asked.
4. **A person may ask for faith-based support and may decline it at any time**, and may change
   their mind, without any effect on their services and without having to give a reason.
5. **A person's religious belief or non-belief is not recorded** in their record unless the
   person has chosen it as part of their own goals and has said it may be written down.
6. **No staff member is required to hold or express any belief** as a condition of employment,
   and none may be pressured to participate in religious activity at work.
7. Any concern that this statement has not been honoured is a rights matter and is handled
   through the grievance process, which anyone may use without retaliation.

*Cross-reference: this statement must also appear in the Rights of Persons Served material
(CARF 1.J) and in the Program Description (CARF 2.A), and be covered at the orientation of
every person served. It is not enough for it to live only here.*

**Because Prayers of Care is already serving people:** everyone currently receiving services
should be given this statement at their next contact, and the orientation record updated to
show it. People already enrolled were oriented before this existed — catching them up is a
short, honest task now, and an awkward gap at survey if it is left.

## Policy

1. Leadership adopts the Mission, Vision and Values in writing, dated and signed.
2. Every employee, contractor, student, intern and volunteer receives them at hire and at
   least annually thereafter, and the receipt is recorded.
3. Every person served is given them at orientation, in plain language.
4. All public material — website, brochures, signage, social media, grant applications and
   referral packets — matches the adopted wording. Nobody publishes a different version.
5. They are reviewed at least annually, and sooner if the services, the population served or
   the operating model changes materially.

## Procedure

1. Leadership reviews this draft and edits the customise notes below.
2. Approver signs the adoption table. **The approval date and the effective date are written
   on the day they actually happen** — never in advance, never in arrears.
3. The document is published where staff and persons served can find it, and any earlier
   version is removed from circulation the same day.
4. Staff are given the adopted version and each signs the acknowledgment. New staff receive it
   during orientation, before independent contact with anyone served.
5. Persons served receive it at orientation; the orientation checklist records that.
6. The owner checks the website and printed material against the adopted wording, and records
   the check.
7. The review is completed by the next review date and recorded in the review table below,
   whether or not anything changed. **"Reviewed, no change" is a complete review — an empty
   review row is not.**
