**Position:** Peer Support Specialist   **Reports to:** {{CLINICAL_SUPERVISOR}}
**Program:** {{PROGRAM_NAME}}   **Status:** [ ] Full time [ ] Part time   **FLSA:** [ ] Exempt [ ] Non-exempt

**Position summary**
Provides non-clinical, recovery-oriented peer support to adults served by {{AGENCY}}, using their own lived experience of a mental health condition, a substance use condition, or both, to support each person's self-directed recovery and full participation in community life.

**Minimum qualifications**
1. Self-identified lived experience of a mental health and/or substance use condition and of recovery from it. **This is a bona fide occupational qualification for this position.**
2. {{PEER_CREDENTIAL}} — held at hire, or attained within [NUMBER] days of hire.
3. High school diploma or equivalent.
4. Valid driver's licence, acceptable driving record, and insured vehicle (if transporting).
5. Ability to pass required background checks and registry screening.
6. Basic computer skills sufficient to document in {{EHR}}.

**Essential functions**
1. Engage people and build a mutual, trusting relationship.
2. Share lived experience purposefully and within a supervised disclosure plan.
3. Support each person to identify their own goals, in their own words, and develop the person-centered plan with them.
4. Support wellness and recovery planning, including the Personal Safety and Crisis Plan.
5. Support skill building for community living, and accompany people into real community settings.
6. Support navigation of benefits, housing, employment, education, health care, and legal matters, teaching self-advocacy rather than doing for.
7. Help build natural and community supports and step back as they take hold.
8. Recognize crisis, respond per policy, and make warm hand-offs.
9. Document every contact accurately in {{EHR}} within {{NOTE_HOURS}} hours.
10. Attend supervision, training, and team meetings; maintain credential and competency.
11. Follow all rights, confidentiality, boundary, safety, and ethics policies.

**Does not include:** assessment, diagnosis, therapy, clinical supervision, medication handling or advice, legal or financial advice.

**Physical and travel requirements:** travel throughout {{COUNTIES}}; drive; enter homes and community locations; occasional lifting of up to [WEIGHT]; work occasional evenings or weekends.
