**Position:** Peer Supervisor   **Reports to:** {{PROGRAM_DIRECTOR}}
**Supervises:** Peer Support Specialists and Senior Peer Support Specialists

**Position summary**
Provides supervision, competency assessment, and development to the peer workforce, and assures the quality and integrity of peer support service and documentation.

**Minimum qualifications**
1. {{PEER_CREDENTIAL}} or the clinical licence required by payer rules — state which: ____________
2. [NUMBER] years of experience delivering or supervising peer support.
3. Supervisory training within [NUMBER] days of appointment.
4. Background checks and registry screening.
5. Where the supervisor is not a certified peer, {{AGENCY}} arranges peer-to-peer consultation for the peer workforce (Policy 1I-04).

**Essential functions**
1. Provide individual supervision twice monthly for the first 90 days and at least monthly thereafter, documented on the Supervision Note.
2. Review self-disclosure, boundaries, and dual relationships at every session.
3. Complete competency assessment at hire and annually, including direct observation of service delivery.
4. Review documentation for timeliness, individualization, goal linkage, and billing integrity.
5. Support staff wellness; maintain and review Peer Wellness Plans.
6. Respond to safety events, conduct debriefings, and support staff after critical incidents.
7. Participate in quality record review and performance improvement.
8. Manage caseload assignment against the staffing plan cap.
9. Complete performance appraisals.

**Physical and travel requirements:** field observation across {{COUNTIES}}; drive; availability for urgent consultation during service hours.
