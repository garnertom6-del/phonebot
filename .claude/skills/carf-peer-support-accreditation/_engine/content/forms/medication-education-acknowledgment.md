**What your peer specialist CANNOT do:**
- Give you medication, hold it, carry it, sort it, or throw it away
- Tell you to start, stop, change, or skip any medication
- Tell you whether a medication is right for you
- Give medical advice of any kind

**What your peer specialist CAN do:**
- Talk about their own experience with medication, as part of their own story
- Help you write down questions to ask your prescriber
- Go with you to an appointment if you want
- Help you set up your own reminder system
- Help you speak up about side effects
- Help you get to a pharmacy or arrange delivery

**Your choice is yours.** {{AGENCY}} respects every recovery pathway. Whether you take medication for mental health or for addiction, or take none, is your decision and your prescriber's. Nobody here will pressure you, and your services will never depend on that choice.

**If you have a problem with a medication** — a side effect, you ran out, you stopped taking it — tell your peer specialist. They will help you contact your prescriber the same day. In an emergency they will call 911.
