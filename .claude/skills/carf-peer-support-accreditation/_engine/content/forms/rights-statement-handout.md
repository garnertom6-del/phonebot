**Your Rights at {{AGENCY}}**

You have the right to:
1. Be treated with dignity and respect, and to be safe from abuse, neglect, exploitation, and retaliation.
2. Be free from restraint, seclusion, and any punishment.
3. Get services in the community, in the least restrictive setting.
4. Be free from discrimination of any kind.
5. Help make your own plan, use your own words for your goals, and say no to anything without losing services.
6. Choose your own recovery pathway — including medication — without pressure.
7. Have your information kept private, and to be told the limits of privacy before you share.
8. See your own record and ask for a correction.
9. Know before services start about any money you may owe.
10. Keep your own money and property.
11. Complain about anything, to anyone, at any time, without it affecting your services.
12. Know who is working with you and what their role is, and to ask for a different worker.
13. Say yes or no to being photographed, recorded, or observed.
14. Ask for help with a disability, and to get information in your language and in a format you can use.
15. Contact anyone you want, in private, at any time — including a lawyer, an advocate, your family, the state, your insurance, or CARF.
