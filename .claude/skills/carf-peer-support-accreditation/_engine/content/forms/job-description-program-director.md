**Position:** Program Director   **Reports to:** {{CEO_TITLE}}
**Supervises:** Peer Supervisor(s) and program staff

**Position summary**
Accountable for the day-to-day operation, quality, safety, and regulatory conformance of {{PROGRAM_NAME}}.

**Minimum qualifications**
1. [DEGREE / CREDENTIAL REQUIRED BY PAYER OR STATE — STATE IT]
2. [NUMBER] years of experience in behavioral health or peer services, including [NUMBER] supervisory.
3. Background checks and registry screening.

**Essential functions**
1. Maintain the written program description and keep practice aligned to it.
2. Maintain the staffing plan and caseload caps; assure coverage.
3. Oversee screening, access timeframes, waitlist, and referral-out.
4. Assure person-centered plans, reviews, crisis plans, and discharge documentation are complete and timely.
5. Oversee incident reporting, safety, drills, and community-visit safety practice.
6. Oversee grievances and rights, and assure orientation of persons served.
7. Participate in quality record review, performance measurement, and improvement.
8. Maintain the Community Resource Directory and partner relationships.
9. Assure staff training, credentialing, supervision, and appraisal occur on schedule.
10. Prepare for and participate in surveys, audits, and licensure inspections.
