I have received, read, and had the opportunity to ask questions about the {{AGENCY}} Code of Ethics (Policy 1A-02). I understand that I am required to follow it in all my work for {{AGENCY}}.

I specifically understand and agree that I will not:
- enter a romantic, sexual, or financial relationship with a person served;
- lend money to, borrow money from, or give money to a person served;
- accept a gift beyond a token of nominal value;
- document a service that did not occur as documented;
- disclose information about a person served without authority;
- misrepresent my credentials or my lived experience.

I understand that I must disclose any conflict of interest and any dual relationship in writing, and that I must report suspected abuse, neglect, exploitation, or fraud without delay. I understand that violation may result in corrective action up to termination and, where required, a report to my credentialing body.
