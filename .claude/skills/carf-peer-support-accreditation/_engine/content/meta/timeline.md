# The 12-Month Roadmap to Survey

**The single most important rule:** CARF wants to see that you have been *doing* this, not that you wrote it last week. Most manuals require the program to have been in operation and generating records for a minimum period before survey, and expect to see data trends and a completed annual analysis. Confirm the exact minimum in your purchased manual and in the CARF Survey Application, then count backwards from there. Everything below assumes a 12-month runway.
