## Purpose
To define how {{AGENCY}} is governed and how the governing authority holds itself accountable.

## Policy
{{AGENCY}} is governed by {{GOVERNING_BODY}}, which holds ultimate responsibility for the organization's mission, financial integrity, legal and ethical conduct, and the quality and safety of services.

## Procedure
1. The duties of {{GOVERNING_BODY}} are set out in writing and include: approving the mission; approving the strategic plan and the annual budget; selecting, supporting, and annually evaluating the {{CEO_TITLE}}; approving major policies; reviewing organizational performance and risk; and ensuring legal and ethical operation.
2. {{GOVERNING_BODY}} meets no less than quarterly. Written minutes record attendance, business conducted, and decisions made, and are retained permanently.
3. Each member completes a conflict of interest disclosure at appointment and annually. A member with a conflict on a matter discloses it and abstains from the discussion and the vote; the abstention is recorded in the minutes.
4. Each member receives a documented orientation covering the mission, the services, the applicable regulations, the financial position, and the member's legal duties.
5. {{GOVERNING_BODY}} evaluates its own performance in writing at least annually and documents actions arising from that evaluation.
6. The {{CEO_TITLE}} is evaluated in writing at least annually against stated expectations. The evaluation is retained in a confidential file.
7. Where {{AGENCY}} has no board and is governed by an owner or sole member, that person performs and documents every function above, and the documentation is dated and signed.

## Evidence the Surveyor Will Ask For
- Written governance duties
- Twelve months of minutes
- Signed conflict of interest disclosures for every member
- The most recent board self-assessment and CEO evaluation
