## 4. Recovery Pathway Neutrality
{{AGENCY}} does not promote any single pathway to recovery. Staff are trained that medication for addiction treatment, psychiatric medication, twelve-step, harm reduction, faith-based, and self-directed recovery are all legitimate. Any staff statement discouraging a person's prescribed medication is a Code of Ethics violation.
