## 1. Purpose
To keep persons served safe and services operating during an event that disrupts normal operations, and to return to normal in an orderly way.

## 4. Prioritizing Persons Served
Persons served are tiered in advance so that outreach happens in the right order. Tiering is reviewed at each plan review.

| Tier | Definition | Contact within |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recent crisis, high risk, unstable housing, medically fragile, lives alone | 24 hours |
| 2 | Active service, stable | 72 hours |
| 3 | Low intensity or nearing transition | 7 days |

## 6. If the Record System Is Unavailable
1. Notify the {{CEO_TITLE}} and the vendor; log the start time.
2. Switch to the paper Downtime Service Log, kept at [LOCATION].
3. Deliver and document service on paper, using the same content requirements.
4. When the system returns, enter paper documentation within [NUMBER] business days, marked as a late entry with both dates.
5. Retain the paper originals per the Records Retention Schedule.
