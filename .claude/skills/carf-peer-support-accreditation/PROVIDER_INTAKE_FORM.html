CARF Provider Intake

Successful Solutions — fill this in once, and the whole accreditation packet is built from it.

Before anything else: the provider must buy the current Behavioral Health Standards Manual from carf.org. Nothing here replaces it, and the packet stays stamped DRAFT until a human checks the section map against the purchased manual.
1. The agency
2. What you are applying for
CARF accredits programs, not job titles. There is no “peer support accreditation.” Peer support is the workforce that delivers a program. A peer-delivered community agency almost always applies for Community Integration.

Only Community Integration content ships today. Anything else stops the builder at the capability gate until the Section 3 content is written.

3. Who you serve and where — these change what gets built

Adolescents and “both” hit the capability gate — that content is not written yet.

4. Operations — these drive the compliance calendar
These multiply the required items. Drills are required on every shift that delivers service; each site needs its own quarterly inspection; each vehicle a monthly one; and supervision, competency, appraisal and wellness plans expand per staff member.
5. Credentials, payers, systems
6. Named people
Leave blank anything you do not know. The builder never invents a name — a blank prints as [FILL IN: role] and lands in the blanks report.

One person may hold several of these. Put the same name in each — the manual records the assignment.

7. Timeframes — your choices, not CARF's
Your state licensure rule or a payer contract may be stricter than these. The strictest one wins. Check before you adopt them.
8. Build 

Fill the form, then press Generate.

Save it as providers/<slug>/provider.json, then run:
python3 _engine/build_provider.py <slug>

Generated documents are drafts for the agency to read, edit, and adopt. This form and the packet it produces contain no CARF standard text — the manual must be purchased from carf.org.
