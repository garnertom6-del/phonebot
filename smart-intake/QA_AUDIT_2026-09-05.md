## Staff-Sent SMS Alternative

The immediate alternative is manual texting from an organization-approved work phone, not another unregistered automated sender. The app now exposes Text from my phone (no Twilio) on the created-intake screen and unfinished case page. Staff can choose iPhone or Android for the correct QR/composer link, or copy the message and use a paired computer app. QR generation stays inside the app; private links are not sent to external QR services.

Computer SMS no longer assumes a desktop has no texting app or cancels every desktop SMS link. Native composer navigation remains in the user's click gesture. Google Messages and Phone Link help open generic URLs without recipient data or private intake tokens. Staff must pair approved devices themselves and manage replies/opt-outs in their chosen app. [Google Messages for web](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/7611075?hl=en), [Microsoft Phone Link](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/phonelink/send-and-receive-text-messages-from-your-pc).

Preparing, copying, scanning, or opening a message is not a send. The manual recording controls require an unchecked-by-default staff confirmation, and their copy explicitly distinguishes a staff report from carrier-confirmed receipt. Loopback links are blocked from manual sharing, including IPv6 localhost. No new vendor account, paid phone number, carrier registration, or messaging permission was created. Phone-side sending and recipient receipt still require a real-device test; this release must not be described as verified SMS delivery.

## SMS Recovery

The observed problem is sender registration, not proof that intake links are forbidden. Twilio documents 30034 as unregistered US long-code traffic. Complete registration and associate the sender with its approved campaign, or use another approved sender type. [Twilio 30034](https://www.twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30034).

The code now supports TWILIO_MESSAGING_SERVICE_SID. When configured, it uses the service's sender pool instead of hard-coding From. A verified toll-free sender is a supported alternative, but still requires Twilio verification; switching vendors or shortening the URL does not remove compliance requirements. [Messaging Services and toll-free prerequisites](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/tutorials/send-messages-with-messaging-services).

Before retrying live: configure the approved sender/service in Render, keep the canonical HTTPS app URL and signed status callback, then send one test and verify the final delivery callback plus handset receipt. Do not repeatedly retry the unregistered sender or use Twilio Verify for intake messages.

Keep health details and raw documents out of SMS. Send the private, expiring intake/copy link; use authorized email or a secure QR as the immediate alternate path. The existing manual SMS option must be used from an organization-approved device and must not be recorded as sent until somebody actually sends it.
